ALU with selector and DMUX outputs from the diagram. Verilog-safe notation: DATA_IN_<0,1,2>[7:0] is implemented as DATA_IN_0, DATA_IN_1, DATA_IN_2. DATA_OUT_<0,1>[15:0], VALID_<0,1>, and PARITY_<0,1> are implemented as DATA_OUT_0, DATA_OUT_1, VALID_0, VALID_1, PARITY_0, PARITY_1. SEL[1:0] selects arithmetic operation: 00 multiplication DATA_IN_0 * DATA_IN_1, 01 subtraction DATA_IN_0 - DATA_IN_1, 10 division DATA_IN_0 / DATA_IN_1, 11 modulus DATA_IN_0 % DATA_IN_1. Division/modulus by zero outputs 0. OUTPUT_CHANNEL for final DMUX routing is SEL[0]. STB and DATA_VALID_IN gate valid output; RES clears outputs.
